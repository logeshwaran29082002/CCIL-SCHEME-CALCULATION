Monthly scheme calculator

Upload this month’s scheme image once, then upload sales data. Slabs and scheme value are calculated for every route.

1. Scheme for the month

Upload the scheme image. Claude reads it and replaces last month’s rules.

Check or edit scheme rules
codes = 4-digit FG numbers. keywords = words matched in product description (for “ALL …” rows). Use + to need several words (glycerine+50ml) and ! to exclude (!25ml). unit = ctn or pcs. branch = blank, only:Dar es Salaam, or except:Dar es Salaam.

2. Sales data

Excel or CSV (loading sheet with item no, description, sold quantity, line amount, location).

3. Scheme result

Add the scheme and sales data to see results.

4. BTL declaration forms

One form per route. Customer and invoice columns stay blank to fill by hand. The date is today’s date, even if you upload return data.
